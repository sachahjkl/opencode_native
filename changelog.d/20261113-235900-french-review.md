**The light theme's fields, French texts and numbers**: in a light theme, the fields, the switches, the sliders' numbers and the choices' tracks are light again instead of black, with their text readable. Menus, the palette, the shortcuts sheet and the composer's hints name the keys as the keyboard of the language prints them ("Ctrl+Maj+N"). Numbers, percents and costs use the language's decimal mark and percent sign ("0,42 $", "34 %" in French). The server's facts start with a capital, "Connecting to…" has a line of its own under the logo in a narrow window, and French texts agree in gender, say "Rechercher" and "Zoom avant" like the other programs.
