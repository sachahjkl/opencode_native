**Updates work as in the other programs of the toolkit.** With "Update by itself" on, opencode native looks for a new version a while after the start and then every day, and installs it: it runs at the next start, or at once from Restart now. The Updates page shows the toolkit's state and its Check now, Install and Restart now buttons; changing the channel (Stable or Beta) takes effect at once. The status bar's button says what there is to do. The channels, the signed manifests and the program downloaded stay the same.
