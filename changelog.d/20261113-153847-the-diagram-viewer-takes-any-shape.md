**The diagram viewer takes any shape**: resize it freely on both axes, always within the window, and the diagram fits it. The wheel moves the diagram, Ctrl with the wheel (on Windows, a pinch on a touchpad too) zooms it, a drag and the arrow keys move it; its buttons zoom in, out, to its size in the conversation and back to fit. A flowchart, a class, state, ER or requirement diagram can be laid out from top to bottom, left to right, bottom to top or right to left.
