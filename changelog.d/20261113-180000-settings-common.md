type: Changed

**The settings are the toolkit's common ones**: the look, the text and fonts, scrolling, input, delays and menus, rendering and updates are the same settings, under the same names, as in the other programs of the toolkit, with a search, an Advanced part, the settings file as a link, and Export and Import. A setting you changed under its old name goes back to its default: `appearance.style` is now `appearance.finish`, the fonts are under `appearance.text`, the density and the glow are numbers, and scroll bars no longer hide by default.
