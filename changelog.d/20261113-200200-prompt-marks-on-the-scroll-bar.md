**Your prompts are marked on the conversation's scroll bar**: the separate rail of dots beside the bar is gone. Each prompt is a thin mark on the scroll bar; the marks of the prompts in view are a little brighter. Prompts too close together share one mark, and the pointer over it lists them. The mark under the pointer grows and shows the prompt; a click goes to it at once. The magnifier above the bar filters the prompts by what you type. The setting is now Settings › Conversation › Prompt marks.
